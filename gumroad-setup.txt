CUTFRAME — GUMROAD SETUP, STEP BY STEP
======================================

The code is already done and sitting in C:\projects\cutframe.
You make 3 products on Gumroad, paste 5 values into two places, push, test.


STEP 1 — Payouts (so the money can reach you)
---------------------------------------------
Gumroad > Settings > Payments
- Country: Albania
- Add your bank details, save.


STEP 2 — Make the 3 products
----------------------------
Gumroad > Products > New product. For EACH one:

  Type:  Digital product
  Name / price:

    Cutframe — Starter, 400 images      $9
    Cutframe — Creator, 1,000 images    $19
    Cutframe — Studio, 3,000 images     $49

Then, on the product's edit page:

  Description (copy this in, change the number per pack):
    400 image credits for Cutframe (cutframe.app). Credits are added to your
    Cutframe account automatically a few seconds after you pay. They never
    expire and there is no subscription.

  Content (what the buyer sees after paying) — add a text block:
    Your credits have been added to your Cutframe account. Go back to
    https://cutframe.app/app/billing to see them. Anything wrong? Email
    support@cutframe.app and include this receipt.

  Settings to check:
    - "Pay what you want": OFF
    - Price in USD
    - Don't create discount codes for these (a discounted sale is refused
      by the site on purpose — it checks the price paid)

  URL / permalink: set it to something clean, e.g.
    cutframe-starter
    cutframe-creator
    cutframe-studio
  (the part after /l/ in the product link). Write down all three.

  Publish each product.


STEP 3 — Tell Gumroad where to send sales (the "Ping")
------------------------------------------------------
Gumroad > Settings > Advanced > Ping endpoint:

    https://cutframe.app/api/gumroad/webhook

Save. (Don't press "Send test ping" expecting credits — test pings are
ignored by design.)


STEP 4 — Get your access token
------------------------------
Gumroad > Settings > Advanced > Applications > Create application
  Name:          Cutframe
  Redirect URI:  https://cutframe.app
Create it, then click "Generate access token". Copy it.

DO NOT paste the token into this chat. It can read every sale on your
account. It only goes in the two places below.


STEP 5 — Put the values in .env.local AND in Vercel
---------------------------------------------------
Open C:\projects\cutframe\.env.local in Notepad and add these lines at the
bottom (use YOUR permalinks from step 2 and YOUR token from step 4):

PAYMENT_PROVIDER=gumroad
GUMROAD_ACCESS_TOKEN=paste-token-here
GUMROAD_PRODUCT_STARTER=cutframe-starter
GUMROAD_PRODUCT_CREATOR=cutframe-creator
GUMROAD_PRODUCT_STUDIO=cutframe-studio

Save.

Then Vercel > your project > Settings > Environment Variables. Add the same
five, one by one (name on the left, value on the right), for Production.
If PAYMENT_PROVIDER already exists (set to paddle or stripe), edit it to
gumroad.


STEP 6 — Push the code
----------------------
Double-click ship.bat in C:\projects\cutframe.
Commit message:  Gumroad payments

Wait for Vercel to finish deploying (Vercel > Deployments, green "Ready").
Pushing also redeploys with the new environment variables.


STEP 7 — Test it with real money ($9)
-------------------------------------
Gumroad treats you buying your own product while logged in as a free
"test purchase", and the site ignores those on purpose. So:

1. Open a private/incognito window, go to cutframe.app, sign in to Cutframe.
2. Credits > Buy on Starter. A Gumroad tab opens.
3. Pay with a real card (yours is fine — just don't be logged in to
   Gumroad in that window).
4. Switch back to the Cutframe tab. Within about a minute: "Payment
   received — 400 credits added".
5. Check History on the billing page shows "Credit pack".
6. If you want the money back: Gumroad > Sales > that sale > Refund.
   Then remove the 400 credits by hand in Supabase (I'll give you the line).

If the credits DON'T appear, don't try again — tell me, and I'll read the
Vercel logs with you. Every failure logs a line starting with
"GUMROAD SALE" saying exactly why.


WHAT THE SITE CHECKS ON EVERY SALE (so you know it's safe)
----------------------------------------------------------
- It never trusts the ping. It asks Gumroad's API "is this sale real?"
  using your token. A faked ping credits nothing.
- The product must be one of your 3 packs, paid, not refunded or disputed,
  and at full price.
- The number of credits comes from the site's own price table, never from
  the request.
- The same sale can only ever be credited once.
- If someone buys straight from your Gumroad page (not through the site),
  it credits the Cutframe account with the same email. If there isn't one,
  it logs "GUMROAD SALE UNATTRIBUTED" and you add it by hand.
